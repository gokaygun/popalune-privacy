Popalune Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

Popalune is a free casual match-3 puzzle game. You do not need to register or provide a name, email address, phone number, or precise location to play.

Information stored on your device

Game progress, settings, selected language, rewards, missions and advertising-frequency state are stored locally on your device. Local progress is the primary save and gameplay can continue offline.

Reminders. If you allow notifications, the game schedules local reminders on your device (for example when your lives are full or a daily reward is ready). They are created on the device; no notification data is sent to us. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in your device settings.

Firebase services and cloud saves

When Firebase is available, Popalune creates a pseudonymous Firebase Anonymous Authentication identifier and uses Cloud Firestore to back up game progression and related game-state data, such as completed levels, stars, scores, virtual currency, inventory, active runs and missions. The game does not ask for a name, email address or phone number for this identifier. Firebase Analytics processes app interaction and gameplay events, such as screens viewed, levels started, completed or failed (with score, stars, moves used and time spent on the attempt), boosters used, rewards claimed and purchases made; Crashlytics processes crash and diagnostic information; and Performance Monitoring processes app startup, performance and network timing information. These services are provided by Google and use data to provide, maintain and improve the game and its services.

Analytics and Crashlytics records are associated with the same pseudonymous Firebase identifier as the cloud save, so that we can understand how players progress (for example the highest level reached), balance difficulty and fix crashes. The cloud save also records session information such as the number of sessions, total play time, the last level and screen, app version and the time the game was last used. This identifier does not reveal your name or contact details.

You can optionally connect Google Play Games in Settings. Play Games authenticates your Google Play Games account and provides a server authorization code used to link or recover your Firebase game identity. Popalune uses the resulting account identifier to restore the cloud save after reinstalling the game. The app does not ask you to enter your Google account email address or name. The authorization code is used for authentication and is not stored in the game save.

Cloud backup requires a network connection and may be unavailable. Without a linked Play Games account, the anonymous identifier may not be recoverable after uninstalling the app or clearing its data.

In-app purchases

Popalune offers optional in-app purchases (coin packs and Remove Ads). Payments are processed by Google Play under Google's terms; Popalune never receives your card or payment details. The game receives the purchased product and an order identifier from Google Play to deliver the purchase. Order identifiers are stored on your device to prevent delivering the same purchase twice, and purchase events are recorded in Firebase Analytics. Purchases are linked to your Google Play account and can be restored with Restore purchases in the shop.

Advertising and consent

Popalune uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for optional rewarded ads and occasional interstitial ads. Unity Ads is an enabled bidding source in the app’s AdMob mediation configuration. These advertising services may process device or advertising identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, ad requests, impressions and interactions, app information and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and service improvement. The advertising providers may receive this information as part of serving or measuring ads.

Where required, Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) presents consent choices before ads are requested and provides a privacy-options form in the game. Your choices and device-level advertising controls can affect ad personalization. Data sent to these providers is transmitted over encrypted network connections.

Data retention and deletion

Delete a Popalune account and its game data in the app: open Popalune, go to Settings, and choose Delete account and cloud data. After you confirm, the app deletes the current player's Firestore cloud-save document and associated Firebase Authentication user, then clears the local game save and session data on that device. It restarts without automatically reconnecting Play Games or creating a new anonymous cloud account. Your Google Play Games account itself is not deleted. You may choose to reconnect it later as a new Popalune game identity.

Request deletion outside the app: email quicksavem@gmail.com with a Popalune account and data deletion request. This works even if you have uninstalled the app. We will tell you what information is needed to identify your game data; do not send a password or authorization code. We can remove the identifiable Popalune Firestore cloud save and its associated Firebase game identity. Local data on a device must be removed on that device.

Popalune does not intentionally retain the deleted cloud save or Firebase game identity after a successful deletion. A failed in-app deletion is reported as a failure, and some cloud data may already have been removed; contact us if a retry does not complete. Analytics, crash, performance and advertising records already processed by Google or an ad provider are not deleted by the in-app control and may be retained under those providers' policies and retention periods.

Children

Popalune is not designed for or marketed specifically to children. Its Google Play target audience is ages 13 and older. Ads in the game are limited to content rated for teens or younger audiences. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete the related game data.

Third-party services

Firebase and AdMob are Google services. Unity Ads is provided by Unity. Their privacy information is available in the Google Privacy Policy and Unity Privacy Policy.
