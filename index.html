Popalune Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026

Popalune is a free match-3 puzzle game. It does not require an account and does not ask you to provide a name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

Information stored on your device

Game progress, settings, selected language, rewards, missions and advertising-frequency state are stored locally on your device using platform preferences. Popalune does not operate a server account for this information.

Advertising and consent

Popalune uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to provide optional rewarded advertisements and occasional interstitial advertisements. Google's advertising services may collect or receive device or other identifiers (including the advertising identifier where available), IP-derived approximate location, ad interaction data, app interaction data, diagnostics and performance information. Google may use and share this information to deliver, measure, prevent fraud in and improve advertising, subject to your consent choices and applicable law.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) presents consent choices before advertising is requested and provides a privacy-options entry in the app. You can also use device-level advertising controls. Data sent to Google is transmitted using encrypted network connections.

Children

Popalune is not designed for or marketed specifically to children. Its Google Play target audience is ages 13 and older.

Data retention and deletion

Local game data can be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Popalune has no user account to delete. Requests concerning data processed by Google are governed by Google's privacy controls and policies.
